The Challenge & Business Overview

Operating a mobile technology retail shop in Medan presents a unique set of operational hurdles. The business handles a complex mix of physical products, technical micro-soldering services, and digital transactions like cash transfers.

Relying on manual ledgers resulted in stock discrepancies, slow checkout times during peak hours, and an inability to track real-time profit margins. To solve this, the core dashboard was designed to give a high-level overview of daily revenue and profit targets instantly.

Offline-First Synchronization

Sporadic internet connectivity disruptions would often completely halt digital logging processes. To combat unreliable internet connectivity, the application utilizes a custom localStorage queuing system.

As seen in the Cashier Module, a persistent "OFFLINE" indicator informs the user. Cashiers can continue processing transactions, logging repairs, and managing inventory entirely offline. Once the connection is restored, a background worker autonomously synchronizes the payload queue with the Google Apps Script backend, ensuring zero data loss.

AI-Powered Receipt Extraction

Processing banking transfers manually is prone to human error and time-consuming. I integrated the Gemini AI API to act as an automated data extractor within the print module.

Users simply upload a photo of a transfer receipt. The AI parses the image to automatically populate the cashier fields (Bank, Account Name, Nominal) via structured JSON. The system then dynamically generates ESC/POS formatted byte arrays and transmits them directly to a 58mm thermal printer via the Web Bluetooth API, providing immediate physical receipts for customers.

Inventory Management

The Warehouse (Gudang) module allows for granular control over physical stock limits, capital prices, and selling prices. It provides an immediate visual warning (Kritis/Habis) to prevent overselling.

Service Desk Integration

To digitize the hardware repair side of the business, a dedicated Service module tracks customer device details, diagnostic issues, and repair statuses, streamlining the workflow from intake to pickup.

Data-Driven Owner Analytics

Utilizing Chart.js, the owner dashboard provides real-time financial insights to inform strategic discounting and hardware procurement.

Revenue Trends

Tracks daily revenue versus profit margins and identifies top-performing product categories.

Operational Heatmap

Visualizes peak operational hours and categorizes slow-moving inventory.

The Learning Process

Building this system pushed my boundaries as a developer. I encountered and resolved complex race conditions where rapid offline inputs could duplicate during synchronization. Fixing this required implementing unique UUIDs for every payload and creating a strict optimistic UI updating pattern.

This project perfectly encapsulates my interdisciplinary approach: applying structured business administration logic to solve real-world problems through innovative front-end web development.
